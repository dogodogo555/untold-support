Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026.

UNTOLD is an anonymous storytelling app. This policy explains what the app and its service collect, why, how long it's kept, and how you can delete it. Questions are welcome at supportuntoldapp@gmail.com.

Who runs UNTOLD

UNTOLD is run by an individual developer, whose legal name appears as the seller on UNTOLD's App Store page. You can reach the developer at supportuntoldapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect this data through the app and our servers when you use UNTOLD, and from what you write or send us.

  • Anonymous account ID. Created automatically through our hosting provider's anonymous sign-in. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, or a password.
  • Agreement record. Which version of the terms you accepted, when, and that you confirmed you're 18 or older.
  • Your stories. The text, category, time, moderation status, and safety flags, plus a fingerprint of the text used to detect duplicates and banned text.
  • Your activity. Hype, reactions, saved stories, authors you block, and reports you send, including any note you add.
  • Usage events. Events such as opening the app, session length, opening or submitting a story and its category, giving Hype or a reaction, creating a share card, and sending a report. They're stored against a salted hash of your account ID and never include story text.
  • Abuse limits. Short-lived records of actions such as posting and reporting, used to enforce limits.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider may process IP addresses, device details, and request details in its logs and sign-in records, and may derive approximate location, such as country or city, from IP addresses. This is for security and running the service, its logs are kept for a limited time, and UNTOLD doesn't use this location information.
  • Support emails. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you send.

What stays on your device

Your sign-in session is kept in this device's Keychain and isn't synced to iCloud. Unsent drafts, the list of stories you've hidden or reported, and app settings are also stored on your device. We don't receive your drafts unless you submit them.

Where the law requires it, the app asks Apple for your age range to check that you're 18 or older. Your age range is used only for that check, on your device. It isn't stored or sent to us.

How we use it

  • To run UNTOLD and show you your own stories and saved items.
  • To enforce one Hype per story, limit abuse, and moderate content.
  • To review reports and respond to safety concerns.
  • To understand how the app is used.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

How moderation works

Every story goes through automatic safety checks, which run on our own database using rules we wrote. Story text isn't sent to an outside moderation or AI company. Depending on the result and our current settings, a story may be published automatically, held for a person to review, or rejected automatically, for example if it contains contact details or looks like spam. Stories the checks flag as a safety concern are held for a person. A moderator may also review published stories and reports.

Who can see what

Other people see only a story's words, category, age, and Hype count. They never see your account ID. The app doesn't show that two stories came from the same person, although blocking an author hides that author's other stories, which could hint that they share a writer.

Authors aren't told who reported them. Moderators see story text, safety flags, report reasons and notes, and how many of an author's stories were approved or rejected. Our moderation tools don't show them an account ID or who sent a report.

The people who operate UNTOLD can technically access the database, including the link between an account ID and its stories.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, anonymous sign-in, and server logs.
  • Apple distributes the app and, where the law requires it, provides the age range check.
  • GitHub hosts this policy's public website, and logs visitors' IP addresses for security.

Supabase's servers may be outside your country, so your data may be transferred abroad.

Providers that process app data for us must protect it to the same or an equal standard as this policy. If you email us, your message is handled by Google, which provides our Gmail account, under Google's own terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

Your stories are sensitive

Stories can reveal things about you, so please leave out identifying details. Once a story is published, others may have read it or shared it as an image, and we can't recall those copies.

How long we keep data

  • Stories and your activity: until you delete them or your account. Stories that were rejected or removed stay in your My Space until then.
  • Usage events: up to 13 months, or until you delete your account.
  • Abuse limit records: about 3 days.
  • Safety copies: see the exception below.
  • Moderation records: we keep reports, moderator decisions, and fingerprints of removed or banned text for as long as needed to prevent abuse.
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to handle your request.

Deleting your data

In My Space you can delete any story, or choose "Delete account and data". Deleting your account also ends your consent to further processing, apart from the records described here, and removes your account, stories, Hype, reactions, saves, blocks, and usage events.

Some records remain. Reports you sent are kept without your account ID, unless the reported story has been deleted. If one of your stories had an open report, a record without its text remains, and its fingerprint stays so it can't be reposted. Earlier moderator decisions about your stories stay in our log with your former account ID, which no longer connects to an account.

There's one safety exception. If a story under review or a serious report involves a possible threat, serious violence, or child safety, a copy of that story may be kept for about 30 days after you delete it, not linked to your account. If a moderator preserves a story for a serious safety or legal reason, a copy linked to your anonymous account ID may be kept for about one year, or longer if the law requires it. Copies are erased after that. Deleted data may also remain in our hosting provider's systems for a short time.

There's no email or password, so if you lose your device, change phones, or delete the app, your account may not be recoverable, and you may not be able to delete your stories yourself. Contact us if that happens, though we may not be able to verify that a story is yours.

Your rights

If Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) applies to you, you can ask us:

  • Whether your personal data is processed, and for what purpose.
  • Whether it's used in line with that purpose.
  • Which third parties receive it, in Turkey or abroad.
  • To correct incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • To erase or destroy your data, under the conditions in the law, and to tell those third parties about a correction or erasure.

You can also object to a result against you that comes solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Send requests to supportuntoldapp@gmail.com. We'll answer as soon as possible, and within 30 days at most. Because accounts are anonymous, we may not be able to confirm that an account is yours, so please use the in-app deletion options where you can. If we refuse a request, answer insufficiently, or don't answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board under the time limits in the law.

You may have other rights under the law where you live.

Advertising and tracking

This version of UNTOLD contains no advertising network, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We'll update this policy before that changes.

Age

UNTOLD is for people aged 18 or older.
