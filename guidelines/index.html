Community Guidelines

UNTOLD is a place for honest stories. Everyone here is trusting strangers, so these rules are strict. Automatic safety checks screen every story. Depending on the result and our current settings, a story may be published automatically, held for a person to review, or rejected. Please report anything that shouldn't be here.

Write about your own life

  • Share your own experiences and feelings.
  • You can discuss identity, faith, and disagreement respectfully, and you can describe what was said or done to you, including hurtful words and abuse you survived or witnessed.
  • Stories must be written by you. No AI-generated, copied, or invented-for-attention content.
  • English only for now.

Never post

  • Harassment, bullying, or anything aimed at a specific person.
  • Threats of violence or harm to anyone.
  • Slurs, insults, or dehumanizing language aimed at people because of their sexual orientation, gender identity, gender, race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, or disability. These protections apply equally to LGBTQ+ people and every other group.
  • Threats against any group, or encouraging violence against them.
  • Justifying or celebrating domestic violence, physical abuse, or sexual abuse.
  • Cruelty to animals, including admitting to, threatening, encouraging, or celebrating it.
  • Sexual content of any kind involving minors, or sexually explicit content in general.
  • Names, addresses, phone numbers, workplaces, social handles, or any details that could identify you or someone else.
  • Spam, advertising, or links.
  • Encouragement of self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or dangerous behavior.

If you're struggling

You can write about pain here. Stories we identify as being about self-harm or suicide get extra care: they're kept out of Trending and Top, and they appear with support resources. UNTOLD isn't a crisis or mental health service. If you may be in danger, please contact your local emergency number.

What happens when rules are broken

We remove stories that break these rules. We have zero tolerance for serious violations, and accounts that post them may be blocked. Reports are reviewed by a person, usually within 24 hours.
