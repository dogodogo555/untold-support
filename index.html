Common questions

How do I report a story?

Tap the three dots on the story, choose Report, pick a reason, and tap Send Report. Reports are private, and the author isn't told who reported them.

How do I hide a story?

Tap the three dots on the story and choose Hide This Story. Hidden stories are remembered on your device.

How do I block an author?

Tap the three dots on one of their stories and choose Block This Author. They won't be told, and you won't learn who they are. To undo every block, go to My Space and choose Unblock all authors.

How do I delete a story?

Open My Space, find the story under My Stories, and delete it.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open My Space and choose Delete account and data. The Privacy Policy explains what is erased and the few records that remain.

I lost my phone, changed phones, or deleted the app

UNTOLD has no email or password, so if you delete the app or change phones, your account may not be recoverable. Write to us, though we may not be able to verify that a story is yours.

If you might be in danger

Please call your local emergency number now.

Talk to someone

  • United States: call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
  • United Kingdom & Ireland: call Samaritans on 116 123.
  • Canada: call or text 988.
  • Australia: call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
  • Anywhere else: find a helpline in your country at findahelpline.com.

Worried about someone else?

Report the story and choose "Someone may be at risk of self-harm". We can't identify or contact the author, but a person will review it.

UNTOLD isn't a crisis service, and nobody here is monitoring stories in real time. If you're struggling, you deserve support from a real person.
